Slimic

Slimic is a captivating, physics-driven platforming puzzle game built using Unity. Take control of a squishy slime ball with custom deformation physics, allowing you to compress, expand, squeeze through tight passages, and stick to various surfaces. Master momentum, trajectory calculations, and structural gravity fields to navigate complex, beautiful environments.
